#### Golang 配置国内镜像源

```
1. go env -w GO111MODULE=auto
2. go env -w GOPROXY=https://goproxy.cn,direct
```

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fab9b11a-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)Golang 配置国内镜像源

#### Idea 下载GoLang运行插件

File-->Settings-->Plugins-->Marketplace 搜索框输入"Go" 点击Installed

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fabf6b82-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)Idea 下载GoLang运行插件

#### 配置Idea Golang运行环境

File-->Settings-->Languages & Frameworks --> Go

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fac55218-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)配置Idea Golang运行环境 - 1

###### 1. 配置GOPATH

添加你的Golang运行目录

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_facc9320-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)配置GOPATH

###### 2. 配置GOROOT

选择你的Golang运行目录

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fad21296-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)配置GOROOT

#### 创建GoLang Project

File --> New --> New Project --> Go

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fad7f828-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)创建GoLang Project 1

点击Next

![](https://oss-emcsprod-public.modb.pro/image/auto/modb_20221215_fadd19de-7c5a-11ed-ac6b-fa163eb4f6be.png)创建GoLang Project 2

输入你的项目名称，选择代码目录
